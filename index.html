❓ Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know

Whether you're ordering a birthday cake or just stopping by for a quick coffee, here are answers to some of our most common questions.

🎂 Do you take custom cake orders?
Yes! We love creating custom cakes for all occasions. Birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events—you name it, we'll bake it. Please place orders at least 3-5 days in advance for best results.
📅 How early should I order a custom cake?
For custom orders, we recommend placing them 5-7 days in advance. This gives our bakers time to perfect your design and ensure the freshest ingredients. Rush orders (2-3 days) are available for an additional charge.
💬 Can I order through WhatsApp?
Absolutely! WhatsApp is our fastest and easiest way to communicate. Send us photos of your cake ideas, ask questions, and confirm orders directly. We're usually online during business hours.
🚚 Do you offer cake delivery in Coimbatore?
Yes, we deliver cakes across Coimbatore city. Delivery charges apply based on distance. Orders must be confirmed at least 2 days in advance for delivery arrangements.
✏️ Can I add a personal message to my cake?
Of course! Personal messages, names, and special dedications are included in our custom cake orders. Let us know exactly what you'd like and how you'd like it written.
⏰ Do you accept same-day orders?
Same-day orders depend on availability and complexity. Our ready-made pastries and simple cakes can sometimes be ready same-day. Best to call or WhatsApp us to check availability.
💳 What payment options are available?
We accept cash, online transfers (UPI, bank transfer), and card payments. For custom orders, we require a 50% advance payment to confirm your booking.
🕐 What are your opening hours?
We're open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Our fresh batches come out at 8:30 AM, so arrive early for the best selection. We're closed only on major national holidays.
